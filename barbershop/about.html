Our Story

Every Cut Is a Craft.

Since 2016, Usta Barbershop has been providing men in Kuala Lumpur with precision, care and a distinctive sense of style.

How It Started

From a Small Shop to a City Favourite

Usta Barbershop started in a small space with one chair and a big dream: to give men more than just a great haircut, but also the confidence to look and feel their best. Today, our team has more than 15 skilled barbers and thousands of happy customers.

Every visit is more than just a haircut. It is an experience — a warm cup of tea, friendly conversation and professional results.
